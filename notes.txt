----------------------------
why is this contract secure: 
----------------------------
It uses msg.sender, so one account cannot vote on behalf of another.
hasVoted[msg.sender] prevents the same address voting twice.
Denied accounts are checked on-chain, not only in JavaScript.
Invalid topic numbers are rejected.
Topics and denied accounts cannot be modified after deployment.
vote() performs all validation before changing state.
It makes no external calls, so there is no practical reentrancy path.
Solidity 0.8 provides checked arithmetic, so vote-count overflow would revert rather than silently wrap.
The function follows the recommended checks-before-effects structure.
